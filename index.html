Our story and Leadership

Delish operates as a part of the LC Family Group franchise, which was founded by Askar and Dana Kaparin.

Built upon a product leadership strategy, our team strives to create products that exceed expectations and set new standards of quality across the city.

Our Uncompromising Product Philosophy

At Delish, we take pride in using strictly natural ingredients and whole farm products. We maintain a zero-tolerance policy towards artificial flavor enhancers, chemical mixtures, or synthetic flavorings. From carefully selected cuts of meat to artisanal farm cheeses, every ingredient is chosen with genuine care for our guests' well-being.

Our beverage line is another point of pride. Our talented baristas participate in annual national coffee championships, winning top places and refining their craft. Instead of commercial syrups, our teas and refreshing lemonades are prepared exclusively from pure fruit and berry purees combined with premium natural ingredients.
